How to complete and turn in this form Hide stepsShow steps
  1. Fill out the form below. Fill this out before each weekly 1:1 with Vince. One form per week. Your answers save automatically in this browser as you type, so you can close the tab and come back later.
  2. Click the "Save as PDF" button at the bottom of the page. A print window opens. Nothing is printing; this is how the PDF gets made.
  3. Set the destination to "Save as PDF", then click Save.
    Chrome or Edge: at the top right of the print window, find Destination (Chrome) or Printer (Edge). If it shows a printer name, click it and choose Save as PDF. Then click the blue Save button.
    Mac Safari: click the PDF menu at the bottom left of the print window and choose Save as PDF.
  4. Save it to your Downloads folder. Don't rename it. A save window opens with the file already named for you, like Firstname_Lastname_Weekly_Review_2026-10-16.pdf. Click Downloads in the list on the left side of that window, then click Save.
  5. Find the PDF you just saved.
    Windows: press Windows key + E to open File Explorer, then click Downloads on the left. Your PDF is at the top of the list under Today.
    Mac: open Finder and click Downloads in the sidebar. Your PDF is at the top.
    Leave this window open for the next step.
  6. Upload it to your folder in Box. Open Box and go to this folder:

    Drag the PDF from your Downloads window into that Box folder. You're done when the file shows up in the folder's list.
    Open Box in a new tab
